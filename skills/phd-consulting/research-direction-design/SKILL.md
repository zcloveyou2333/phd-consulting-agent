# Research Direction Design

Generate PhD research direction options based on the student's background, preferences, constraints, target regions, and career goals.

Always separate student-provided facts, extracted document facts, verified public facts, model-generated strategy, and simulated examples.

Output in practical Chinese consulting language with:

1. 理论型方向
2. 应用型方向
3. 交叉型方向
4. 每个方向的匹配理由
5. 每个方向的风险和避坑点
6. 适合投递的学院或学科口径
7. 可以直接对学生解释的话术

Respect constraints such as avoiding quantitative work, policy, education, technical AI, or other areas the student dislikes.
